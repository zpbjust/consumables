## Description

```text
Stop guessing which filter, bulb, battery, or appliance part your home needs.

HomeParts keeps the exact product details, location, replacement schedule, stock, and shopping information for the things your household uses up. Everything stays organized by home and room, ready when you are standing in the hardware-store aisle or checking what needs attention next.

SAVE THE EXACT REPLACEMENT
• Record the name, category, brand, model number, and size
• Add a photo or scan a product label with on-device text recognition
• Save the home, room, and exact location where the item belongs
• Keep the preferred store, typical price, product link, and notes

STAY AHEAD OF REPLACEMENTS
• Set a replacement interval and last-replaced date
• See overdue, coming-up, and later items in one schedule
• Browse scheduled replacements on a calendar
• Receive local reminders for items you choose
• Mark an item as replaced and keep its history
• Record the date, quantity, price, store, and notes for each replacement

MANAGE STOCK AND SHOPPING
• Track how many replacements you already have at home
• Add saved items to a shopping list without retyping model or size
• Keep purchase quantity and preferred store with each shopping entry
• Mark purchases as bought to add them to your stock

ORGANIZE YOUR HOME
• Group items by home and room
• Search and filter saved items
• Export a backup with photos and import it when needed
• Export replacement history as a CSV file
• Use the core workflow without an account

PRIVACY BY DESIGN
Your homes, rooms, item details, photos, schedules, shopping list, and replacement history are stored on your device. Product-label recognition is processed on your device with Apple Vision. HomeParts does not use advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking.

HOMEPARTS PRO
The free version supports up to 5 saved items and 1 home. HomeParts Pro is a one-time Lifetime Unlock that adds:
• Unlimited saved items
• Multiple homes
• Permanent Pro access with no recurring subscription

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when the purchase is confirmed. The Lifetime Unlock is a one-time purchase and does not renew. You can restore the purchase on devices using the same Apple Account.
```
